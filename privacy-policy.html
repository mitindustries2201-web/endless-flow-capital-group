Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Endless Flow Capital Group ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website endlessflowcapitalgroup.com or engage with our AI automation and business services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and business name submitted via our contact form.
  • Business Information: Optional business name and website details submitted through the contact form.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site, and referral source collected automatically.
  • Communications: Messages, emails, and chat interactions you send us.
  • Diagnostic Responses: AI Business Audit responses and full diagnostic results are processed in the browser on your device and may be saved locally by your browser.
  • Diagnostic Summary Handoff: If you choose to include it with a contact inquiry, a limited summary of diagnostic output may be submitted with your form.
  • Clipping Job Data: Authorized uploaded/imported media, generated transcripts, intermediate files, and rendered clip outputs used for the AI Video Clipping Application workflow.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Generate your on-device diagnostic output from AI Business Audit responses
  • Respond to inquiries and provide client support
  • Process a limited diagnostic summary only when you intentionally include it with your inquiry
  • Process authorized media into short clips and related transcript outputs
  • Send service updates, proposals, and relevant communications
  • Analyze website traffic and optimize user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party platforms to operate this website, including but not limited to:

  • Hosting and Deployment: Services used to host and publish the site
  • Form Processing: Services configured to process contact-form submissions
  • Analytics: Traffic and behavior analytics tools

Each third party has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data. We recommend reviewing those policies.

When included, diagnostic summary data is self-reported and non-authoritative.

5. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies and similar technologies for core functionality or analytics. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards for this website and configured services. However, no internet transmission method is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

Contact-form submission retention depends on the configured form-processing providers and operational requirements. AI Business Audit diagnostic data remain in browser storage on your device until cleared.

Only a limited diagnostic summary and submitted contact fields are processed when you intentionally include summary data in a contact inquiry.

AI Video Clipping Application job files are temporary and retained according to configured cleanup policy (up to 24 hours in this MVP) or until explicit deletion.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@endlessflowcapitalgroup.com.

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.
