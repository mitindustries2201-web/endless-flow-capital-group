01 · The Business Leak Problem

Most growth problems are system leaks, not traffic shortages.

Where momentum gets lost

  • Disconnected systems that do not share data.
  • Slow lead response and inconsistent follow-up.
  • Weak lead capture and unclear pipelines.
  • Poor customer data quality and reporting blind spots.

What this creates

  • Owner dependency for routine decisions and tasks.
  • Operational bottlenecks that throttle growth.
  • Missed conversion opportunities inside current demand.
  • Uncertainty about what should be fixed first.
