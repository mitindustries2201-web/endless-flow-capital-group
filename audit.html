Diagnosis Before Prescription

The diagnosis comes first. The solution comes second.

  • A low website score does not automatically mean “buy a website.”
  • A low acquisition score does not automatically mean “run ads.”
  • A low FlowScale score does not automatically determine a package.

FlowCore evaluates the active constraint, business complexity, existing systems, economics, urgency, capacity, and customer-journey realities before recommending next actions.
